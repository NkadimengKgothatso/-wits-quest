    User Feedback¶

    How Team Big-O collects and acts on feedback from real players — the survey instrument, what it asks, and how responses feed each sprint's retrospective and the next sprint's backlog. The deliverable is evidence of a real feedback loop: a structured instrument, real respondents, and documented outcomes across two rounds.

    Jump to: Sprint 2 feedback · Sprint 3 feedback · Overall feedback

    The feedback form¶

    ➡️ Wits Quest — User Feedback (Google Form)

    The form is distributed to students who played the live game (coursemates, dorm-mates, and students met on campus at actual landmark locations). It combines scaled ratings (1–5), multiple choice, and free-text questions so we can quantify usability trends while still catching bugs only a real player would hit. The same 21 questions were used in both rounds, so Sprint 3 results map one-to-one onto Sprint 2.

    What the form asks¶

    The 21 questions are grouped to mirror the player's journey through the game:

    Background (who is answering)

    1. Have you played Wits Quest before? (multiple choice)
    2. Have you ever played a location-based game before (e.g. Pokémon GO, Ingress)? (multiple choice)
    3. How would you describe your experience with mobile or web games? (1–5, Novice → Expert)
    4. How comfortable are you trying out new apps or software? (1–5)
    5. What device did you play on? (multiple choice)

    Onboarding

    1. How easy was it to sign up and get started? (1–5)
    2. Did anything block or confuse you when signing up or logging in? (multiple choice)
    3. If you ran into any setup problems, please describe what happened. (short answer, optional)

    Core gameplay

    1. How easy was it to find your way around the campus map? (1–5)
    2. Did the location-based trivia unlock when you reached a landmark? (multiple choice)
    3. How fun are the card battles against the CPU? (1–5)
    4. How fair did the CPU battles feel? (1–5)
    5. Did you collect any cards? (multiple choice)
    6. How motivating are the rewards (XP, Essence, new cards)? (1–5)

    Presentation & performance

    1. How would you rate the visual design and adventure theme? (1–5)
    2. Did the game feel fast or slow? (multiple choice)
    3. Was the text readable and were buttons easy to tap? (multiple choice)

    Outcomes

    1. What is the ONE thing you would change or improve? (short answer)
    2. How likely are you to recommend Wits Quest to another student? (1–5)
    3. Did you find any bugs or broken features? Please describe. (short answer, optional)
    4. Anything else you'd like to tell us? (short answer, optional)

    Sign-up / OTP verification is still the top blocker. Codes are rejected as incorrect or expired, the resend button does nothing, and login often only works after a retry or refresh. Q7 — 4 of 5 hit a verification or login problem; Q6 — 3 of 5 rated sign-up 2/5; Q8 and Q20 quotes Stays #1 on the backlog; add reproduction steps to the Bug Tracker. Recurring from Sprint 2

    Trivia scrolls in the wrong direction. Swiping up moves the content up instead of down. Q20 — trivia swipe direction reported New bug in the Bug Tracker
    Header buttons appear not to respond. Q20 — "the buttons in the header tap don't work" New bug; confirm on Android and check for a missing handler
    Trivia needs more variety and replayability. Players finish the events once and have nothing left but battles. Q18 — make trivia more fun, add more questions, allow multiple plays Content and replay-loop task for the next sprint
    Trivia geofence unlock is improving. Q10 — 40% "every time", 20% "sometimes", 0% "never" (Sprint 2: 0% / 40% / 20%) Keep monitoring; 2 of 5 didn't try the trivia
    Battles need sound and slightly larger text. Q18 — bigger font, sound during battles Polish backlog. Q17 was 100% "all good", so low priority
    The core loop and look land well. Exploring campus, rewards and the leaderboard are motivating; sign-up input validation was praised. Q15 — 4 of 5 rated 5/5; Q14 — 4 of 5 rated 4/5; Q19 — 80% rated 4 or 5; Q21 quotes Keep as-is; cite in the Sprint 3 retrospective
    Performance is solid. Q16 — 80% "fast and responsive"; Q17 — 100% "all good" No action needed; note in the retrospective

    The location-privacy concern from Sprint 2 was not repeated in this round, but it hasn't been confirmed as resolved, so it stays on the review list.

    What the overall picture tells us¶

    Signal Evidence Where it goes
    Onboarding is the biggest drag on the experience. Verification failures affect most players, and they are the first thing new players meet. Q7 — 8 of 10 hit a problem; Q6 — average 2.9/5, and no player rated sign-up 5/5 in Sprint 2 Priority #1 on the backlog; track in the Bug Tracker
    Players don't know what to do once they're in. Instructions were requested in both rounds, and most players never reached the card loop. Q18, Q20; Q13 — 6 of 9 didn't get that far Tutorial / how-to-play task, including battle rules
    The core idea works. Design, rewards and exploring campus score well, and half of players volunteered praise. Q15 — average 4.1/5; Q14 — average 3.8/5; Q19 — 6 of 10 rated 4 or 5 Keep as-is; highlight in the retrospective
    Trivia needs work on both reliability and content. Unlocking is improving but still inconsistent, and finished events leave nothing to replay. Q10; Q18 trivia comments Geofence re-tuning plus replayable trivia content
    Battles are middling. Fun and fairness sit around the middle of the scale, and players want clearer rules. Q11 — average 3.1/5; Q12 — average 3.2/5 CPU difficulty tuning and the CPU Battle Rulebook
    Performance and readability are solid. Q16 — 7 of 10 "fast and responsive", none "very slow"; Q17 — 9 of 10 "all good" No action needed

    Suggested priorities for the next sprint¶

    3. Improve trivia: geofence reliability, swipe direction, more variety and replayability.
    4. Investigate the non-responsive header buttons.
    5. Review location-sharing defaults.
    6. Polish: larger text option and battle sound.
