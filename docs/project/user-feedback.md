# User Feedback

How Team Big-O collects and acts on feedback from real players — the survey instrument, what it asks, and how responses feed the Sprint 2 retrospective and the Sprint 3 backlog. The deliverable is evidence of a real feedback loop: a structured instrument, real respondents, and documented outcomes.

---

## The feedback form

**➡️ [Wits Quest — User Feedback (Google Form)](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScZaBww5ClrueNKgGNbBI0dB1FSKyLwNcZw0eFAeAn80-zldw/viewform)**

The form is distributed to students who played the game during the Sprint 2 testing window (coursemates, dorm-mates, and students met on campus at actual landmark locations). It combines scaled ratings (1–5), multiple choice, and free-text questions so we can quantify usability trends while still catching bugs only a real player would hit.

## What the form asks

The 21 questions are grouped to mirror the player's journey through the game:

**Background (who is answering)**

1. Have you played Wits Quest before? *(multiple choice)*
2. Have you ever played a location-based game before (e.g. Pokémon GO, Ingress)? *(multiple choice)*
3. How would you describe your experience with mobile or web games? *(1–5, Novice → Expert)*
4. How comfortable are you trying out new apps or software? *(1–5)*
5. What device did you play on? *(multiple choice)*

**Onboarding**

6. How easy was it to sign up and get started? *(1–5)*
7. Did anything block or confuse you when signing up or logging in? *(multiple choice)*
8. If you ran into any setup problems, please describe what happened. *(short answer, optional)*

**Core gameplay**

9. How easy was it to find your way around the campus map? *(1–5)*
10. Did the location-based trivia unlock when you reached a landmark? *(multiple choice)*
11. How fun are the card battles against the CPU? *(1–5)*
12. How fair did the CPU battles feel? *(1–5)*
13. Did you collect any cards? *(multiple choice)*
14. How motivating are the rewards (XP, Essence, new cards)? *(1–5)*

**Presentation & performance**

15. How would you rate the visual design and adventure theme? *(1–5)*
16. Did the game feel fast or slow? *(multiple choice)*
17. Was the text readable and were buttons easy to tap? *(multiple choice)*

**Outcomes**

18. What is the ONE thing you would change or improve? *(short answer)*
19. How likely are you to recommend Wits Quest to another student? *(1–5)*
20. Did you find any bugs or broken features? Please describe. *(short answer, optional)*
21. Anything else you'd like to tell us? *(short answer, optional)*

## What the responses tell us

| Signal | Evidence | Where it goes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sign-up verification is the top blocker.** Codes were rejected as "incorrect", and one player found a refresh loophole that bypasses verification entirely. | Q6 — nobody rated sign-up above 3/5; Q7 — 40% hit the verification email; Q8 & Q18 quotes | #1 item on the Sprint 3 backlog, tracked in the [Bug Tracker](../development/bug-tracking.md) |
| **Players need instructions on how to play.** | Q18 — "Give instructions on how to play" | Onboarding/tutorial task for Sprint 3 |
| **Location privacy concern.** | Q18 — "your location being public to everyone" | Review location-sharing defaults in Sprint 3 |
| **Trivia geofence unlock is unreliable.** | Q10 — 0% "every time", 40% "sometimes", 20% "never" | Geofence radius and landmark re-tuning |
| **The core loop hooks players.** Campus exploration plus reward-based ranking was called "very addictive". | Q18 praise quote; Q19 — 40% very likely to recommend | Keep as-is; cite in the Sprint 2 retrospective |
| **Performance and readability are solid.** | Q16 — 60% "fast and responsive"; Q17 — 80% "all good" | No action needed; note in the retrospective |

## How responses are used

| Feedback stream | Feeds into |
| :--- | :--- |
| Bugs reported (Q20) and setup problems (Q8) | The [Bug Tracker](../development/bug-tracking.md) and fix register, with reproduction steps |
| Battle fun/fairness ratings (Q11–Q12) | CPU AI difficulty tuning and the [CPU Battle Rulebook](../development/cpu-battle-rules.md) |
| Sign-up friction (Q6–Q8) | Auth flow refinements following the Supabase Auth migration |
| Map and landmark questions (Q9–Q10) | Geofence radius and landmark placement decisions |
| Recommend score (Q19) | The overall Sprint 2 retrospective and Sprint 3 scope selection |
