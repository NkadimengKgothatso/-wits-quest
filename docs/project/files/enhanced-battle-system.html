Feature proposal · Battle system

Let the cards fight

Right now a battle is two cards and a number comparison. This proposal keeps every card rule we already have, but turns your deck into an avatar that fights turn by turn on screen. You still pick the moves. The cards still decide what happens.

The idea

The avatar is just the cards, drawn

Under the hood

The server decides, the browser just shows it

We already moved battle logic server-side in Sprint 3. This keeps it there. The animation is only allowed to play what the server has already resolved, so nobody can change a result from their browser.

Server (battle engine)

  • Whose turn it is
  • Whether the chosen action is allowed
  • Card attributes and ability effects
  • Damage, shields and remaining HP
  • Victory, defeat and saving the match

Client (React app)

  • Sends the chosen action
  • Plays the attack, shield or KO animation
  • Updates HP bars from the server's numbers
  • Shows the battle log and final result
turnResult = resolveTurn(battleState, playerId, action) └─ calculateDamage(attacker, defender, ability) = ATK × 3 × 100 / (100 + DEF) × ability bonus × shield factor └─ returns new HP, effects, and an event list the client animates
Categories

Each category fights differently

Card categories are mostly labels today. Here they give the avatar a playstyle, so mixing categories becomes a real choice.

Rarity

Rarer cards hit harder, but not too hard

The brief asks for rarity levels. Rarity unlocks better abilities, while stat ranges overlap so a common deck is never hopeless.

The goal: a player who reads the battle well, with a sensible deck, should be able to beat someone who just owns rarer cards. Abilities have cooldowns and stats are averaged across the deck, which stops one Legendary from carrying everything.

CPU opponents with a bit of personality

The difficulty picker in the demo above uses these three rules.

Medium

Looks at its own health and uses its special when it is ready.

Hard

  • Shields before your special lands
  • Saves its special to finish you off

Decisions

What this proposal commits to, and what the team still needs to decide

Proposed

Keep it turn-based. The brief says turn-based, and a real-time fighter would be a different game. Animations play between turns.

Proposed

Cards stay the source of truth. The avatar never has stats of its own. If you remove the visuals, the battle still works.

Proposed

Battle logic stays on the server. Same reason we moved battles server-side in Sprint 3: the client can't be trusted with results.

Open

What the avatar looks like. Options are a Wits student, a mascot creature, or a historical figure per category. Needs a quick team vote.

Open

How we balance rarity. The averaging and cooldown idea needs playtesting with real card data before we lock numbers.

Open

Scope for this sprint. We could ship the CPU version first and add avatars to PvP once it feels right.
